What went live — MP interstitial A/B experiment
What changed in test
Test digits (0,1) received the new MP "Most Loved" interstitial — RTB-first design with product grid (Taaza Paneer, Ghar Jaisa Dahi, Idli Batter, Breads, Eggs). Control digits (2,3) saw existing B&B interstitial. Fires post-FO for trial users.

The 4+4 banner height was also shortened in test — making MP listicles visible in the first viewport. That change is about Milk, not MP, so any MP delta from it is incremental visibility gain, not campaign effect.

What we're measuring — and why
Primary metric: MP delivery count (DCC) per digit — because more MP buyers is what ultimately matters for retention, not CTR alone.

Secondary: impressions, CTR, VTP (view-to-purchase). CTR% alone can mislead — a broader audience will naturally have lower per-impression rate. Absolute buyer count > conversion rate.

What the data actually shows
More impressions, lower CTR% — but absolute buyer count matters more than the rate. A campaign shown to a broader audience will naturally have lower per-impression CTR.

Investigating whether combined interstitial is generating more total MP buyers despite the lower rate.
SKU signals: Taaza Paneer 180g (+25.6% CC) and Ghar Jaisa Dahi 400g (+10.9%) are the strongest gainers — both winning with lower discount than decliners. Quality and trust signal stronger than price for MP. Consistent with the RTB-first design intent.
What I don't know yet
🥛
Why is Milk buyer share declining?
D7 buyer share fell 18.4% → 15.9% over 5 months while buyer retention held flat. Is it a discovery problem, a pricing problem, or are newer cohorts just less milk-first? The answer changes what to do next entirely.
Needs source-level data
🧈
Is Ghee's collapse permanent or fixable?
400ml entry SKU pricing instability (₹82–₹259 SP swings) is the likely cause. But has the buyer profile shifted? Are users buying Ghee now fundamentally different from Aug–Sep cohorts? Pricing alone may not recover the signal.
Needs cohort composition analysis
🥜
What does it take to move Snacks reach from 0.8% to 2%?
Highest D28 delta in the portfolio at 11.9pp. At 2% buyer share, Snacks retention contribution would rival Milk's. This has never been tested. The wireframes push Snacks into week-1 flow — but the unlock (discovery vs pricing vs specific SKU) is unknown.
Highest-leverage untested lever
Looking ahead

Mapping the full customer communication journey — from first-order trigger to 180-day retention — and identify exactly where the right message, at the right moment, could move the needle on each of the signals we found.
